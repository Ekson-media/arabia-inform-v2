Our Story

From Vision to a Living Arabic Archive

In 1997, Arabia Inform began with a singular conviction: that the Arabic-speaking world's knowledge — its news, scholarship, broadcast, and intellectual heritage — must be captured, preserved, and made accessible. Not partially. Not selectively. Completely.

Operating from two dedicated processing centers running 24/7, Arabia Inform has never missed a single day of coverage in over 29 years. What began as a media clipping service has become the world's most comprehensive repository of Arabic knowledge — spanning 3+ billion online articles, 280+ million print clips, 120+ million broadcast hours, and 1.7 million academic papers.

Today, Arabia Inform serves governments, universities, research institutions, corporations, and AI companies across the globe — providing the data, intelligence, and historical context that powers decisions, shapes policy, and advances Arabic language technology.

29 Years of Growth

Our Journey

From Archiving Knowledge to AI-Powered Intelligence

1997: Company Founded

Arabia Inform begins with a singular vision to capture, preserve, and provide intelligent access to the Arabic-speaking world's knowledge.

1998: Operations Begin

The first print media archive is successfully launched, establishing the foundation of daily continuous coverage without missing a single beat.

2004: Online Archive Begins

As digital media rapidly expands, Arabia Inform begins comprehensive web content monitoring to adapt to modern publishing landscapes.

2006: Broadcast Archive

Integration of television and radio monitoring introduces real-time logging, analysis, and archiving of spoken Arabic words across channels.

2009: YouTube Integration

Responding to the rise of independent media and user-generated content, video insights and YouTube archiving are formalized into the system.

2010s: Academic Expansion

Massive incorporation of research papers, dissertations, and intellectual history transforms the archive into an academic engine.

2020+: AI Readiness

Advanced NLP datasets and high-speed API infrastructure position Arabia Inform as the leading backbone for Arabic Artificial Intelligence training.

2024: 3+ Billion Articles

A historic scale milestone is achieved as the database crosses 3 billion indexed, cleaned, and searchable Arabic sources.

2026: Arabia Inform Today

Undisputedly the largest Arabic archive in existence. We continuously curate the present to preserve the future of the Arab world.
